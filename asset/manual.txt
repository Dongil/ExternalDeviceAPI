1. Introduction

This 4K30 HDMI Seamless Matrix is a perfect solution for video transmission from 8 HDMI
sources to 8 HDTV displays. It supports seamless switching and video wall functionality.
8 channel inputs support video resolution up to 4K30Hz. 8 channel outputs support
independent resolution modification, HDMI and DVI output switching, brightness and
contrast adjustment. This matrix supports scene preset, saving, calling, deleting and polling.
Advanced EDID management is supported.
This matrix can be controlled through the front panel buttons, IR remote control, RS-232
commands and Web GUI.

2. Features

 Supports 10.2Gbps video bandwidth
 Features 2 operational modes:

    - 8×8 Matrix (seamless switching)
    - Video wall (4×2, 2×2 or 1×8 etc configuration)
 8 channel inputs support video resolution up to 4K30Hz, as specified in HDMI 1.4b
 8 channel outputs support independent resolution modification:
    1024x768@60Hz, 1280x720@50Hz, 1280x720@60Hz, 1280x1024@60Hz,
    1360x768@60Hz, 1440x900@60Hz, 1600x900@60Hz, 1600x1200@60Hz,
    1920x1080@50Hz, 1920x1080@60Hz, 1920x1200@60Hz, 3840x2160@30Hz,
    4096x2160@30Hz
 8 channel outputs support HDMI and DVI output switching
 8 channel outputs support brightness and contrast adjustment
 Advanced EDID management, supporting preset EDID, copy EDID, custom EDID (upload)
 Up to 16 preset scenes, supporting preset scene save, call, delete, polling (the polling
    time can be set)
 Control via the front panel buttons, IR remote control, RS-232 commands and Web GUI

Before connecting and logging into the Web GUI, it is necessary to obtain the IP address of the
